## Prompt

【Generation Goal】 One continuous 14-second handheld 16:9 take, no cuts, that looks filmed by a friend dancing along on a city street: six friends, two of them talking to the camera, and a giant bear walking upright past behind them, so big that only its two shaggy legs are ever in frame. Clean frame: no REC, timestamp, focus brackets, grid, border, subtitles, text.
【Reference Asset Roles】 @image1 = the first frame: starting composition, the four people in it, their outfits and the street. PINKDRESS, GREYMAN and the bear are not in it; they enter later. @image2 = LEADER: face, black blunt bob with short fringe, off-white pinstripe yukata with wide charcoal obi only; not its grey background. @image3 = HERO: face, platinum blonde hair, black scoop tank, white pleated trousers, black Mary Janes only; empty hands. @image4 = FEATHER: face, long wavy black hair, lavender feather dress only. @image5 = SILVERMAN: face, shoulder-length silver hair, white crew tee, beige chinos, black belt only. In each cast image use only the face on the left; the right panels show clothes only. @image6 = PINKDRESS: face, long wavy chestnut-brown hair, pink sleeveless V-neck fit-and-flare mini dress, pearl drop earrings, nude pumps only. @image7 = GREYMAN: face only. He is a short man, about 165 cm, his head about level with PINKDRESS's eyes. He is not in the first frame, so his look comes from this text: short neatly cropped silver-grey hair close to the head, thin round light-metal glasses, plain black crew-neck tee with no logo or mark, black jeans, black belt, black sneakers with white soles; not the hair, glasses or clothes of @image7. @image8 = LOCATION: the street, buildings, crosswalk and curb only; no people, no furniture. @audio1 = LEADER's voice: her timbre, accent, pitch and delivery, and at its end her pronunciation of "Higgsfield" (HIGGS-feeld). It belongs to LEADER only. @audio2 = HERO's voice: her timbre, accent, pitch and tone, and in its second half her pronunciation of "Higgsfield" (HIGGS-feeld) and of the prices "nine dollars forty-five" and "one dollar eighty". Do not copy its other words or its timing. It belongs to HERO only. @audio3 = pronunciation guide only, in HERO's voice: exactly how to say "Higgsfield" (HIGGS-feeld) and "treg" (TREG, rhymes with egg and Greg). Copy only how these two words sound; do not copy any other words, timing or delivery from them.
【Subjects and Relationships】 Six people, no one else: LEADER, HERO, FEATHER, SILVERMAN, PINKDRESS, GREYMAN, plus one giant bear. Each outfit belongs only to its owner. HERO stays frame-left of LEADER. The group is tight and close to the camera, like friends hanging out together, not spread across the street: nobody is more than about 3 m from the lens. FEATHER is about 1.5–2 m back, just behind LEADER and HERO. PINKDRESS and GREYMAN are not in frame at the start; they walk in from the left at about 3 s, close behind the group, about 1–2 m behind LEADER, and walk across to the right. The bear walks the same path about 5 m further back than the couple, behind everyone. SILVERMAN starts at frame-left about 1.5 m back. The dancers drift a little closer over the take, so the group stays tight around LEADER and HERO.
【The Bear】 A giant brown bear walking upright on its two hind legs, like a person, so huge that only its two legs fit in the frame, cut off by the top edge of the frame above the knees. Its head, body, belly and front paws are never seen. Its fur is extremely long and shaggy, about twice as long as a normal bear's, hanging down in thick dark-brown locks that sway, bounce and shake heavily with every step. No claws are visible: the long fur covers the feet, and only the soft dark sole of a foot shows for a moment when it lifts. It strides from left to right, the two legs crossing one in front of the other like someone walking past, each foot landing flat with its full weight on the wet asphalt; puddles ripple and splash a little. It ignores everyone and keeps walking.
【Location】 The empty city street of @image8: pale stone cast-iron building fronts with fluted columns and warm-lit shop windows, wet dark asphalt, a white zebra crosswalk, a granite curb. No furniture.
【Event Script】
0.0–0.6 s: LEADER dances right up to the lens, laughing, hips swaying, bob swinging, and swings one arm in a quick wave right in front of the camera so her wide pinstripe sleeve sweeps across the lens and actually brushes against it: for a split second the soft out-of-focus striped fabric fills the frame and the camera gives a small bump, then the sleeve slides off and her face is back; silent.
0.6–2.3 s: LEADER stops dancing, stands still and says "Still paying Higgs-field for this?" to the lens, sly raised eyebrow; the camera steps back to collarbone-up, focus locks on her eyes. HERO soft at frame-left, dancing and watching her.
2.5–4.8 s: the camera pulls back to about 2 m, waist-up, LEADER dancing beside HERO; a quick hand pan to HERO, who stops dancing, stands still and says "We made Higgs-field three x cheaper." toward the lens. LEADER grins at her. At about 3 s PINKDRESS and GREYMAN walk into frame from the left, about 1–2 m behind LEADER, shoulder to shoulder, strolling slowly toward the right; FEATHER dances on her own; SILVERMAN dances closer, watching HERO.
5.0–9.8 s: medium-wide at about 1.5 m, HERO stands still, not dancing, and says "I cost nine dollars forty-five on Higgs-field… and now one dollar eighty on treg.", counting on her fingers at chest height; LEADER dances beside her and reacts a beat late with a delighted look. At about 7.5 s GREYMAN spots the lens; both quickly cover their faces with their hands and sneak off fast out of frame-right by about 9.5 s. From about 5.5 s the bear's two giant long-haired legs stride into frame from the left, upright, behind the couple, crossing one in front of the other, the long fur shaking with each step; each step lands with a deep thud and the camera gives a tiny jolt. The bear keeps striding right.
9.9–10.6 s: SILVERMAN dances across right in front of the lens from left to right, arms loose and swinging, mid-groove, not noticing the camera; his white tee fills and blocks the whole frame for a moment, dark and blurred, autofocus hunts, then he is gone.
10.7–13.1 s: he clears the frame; LEADER leans right into the lens until her face fills the frame, about 0.3 m away, eyes level with the camera, and says "Told you. It's pay per call." with a sly, knowing smirk: a closed-lip smile pulled up on one side, narrowed eyes, one eyebrow slightly raised, chin a little down, like someone who was right all along. No laugh, no open-mouth grin. HERO dancing and laughing soft behind her shoulder. Behind them, soft and out of focus, the bear's last shaggy leg lifts and strides out of frame-right by about 12 s.
13.1–13.5 s: still smirking, LEADER reaches toward the camera with an open palm; her hand grows until it fills the whole frame.
13.5–14.0 s: her palm covers the entire lens. The frame is completely black and stays fully black to the last frame: no gaps of light between the fingers, no partial cover, nothing visible.
【Camera and Light】 Fujifilm X100VI 23mm f/2, Eterna film look: low saturation, soft contrast, lifted shadows, fine grain, shallow depth of field. Held in one hand by a friend dancing along: loose bounce on the beat, small tremors, framing that drifts and corrects, a slight horizon tilt he keeps fixing, no gimbal, no smooth gliding; he moves close to whoever speaks and backs off to show the group. While the bear walks, each of its steps gives the camera a tiny jolt. Soft overcast late-afternoon daylight around 6500K from a white-grey sky, no direct sun, no hard shadows; shop windows add a small warm 2700K glow in the background only. A light breeze moves hair, sleeves and feathers.
【Performance】 Movement style for everyone, like a group of friends goofing around backstage before a show: loose, carefree, unpolished party dancing, never a routine. Typical moves: hips swaying side to side, shoulders rolling, one or both arms lifted overhead and waving loosely, wrists flicking, a lazy half spin on the spot, walking-while-dancing (strolling a few steps with a bounce, then stopping to groove), playful hand flourishes toward each other, heads tilted back laughing. Moves are big enough to read clearly but relaxed and a little sloppy, each person in their own rhythm, nobody in sync. LEADER and HERO: whenever she is not speaking, each of them dances this way. Whenever she is speaking, she stops dancing and stands still, talking like a normal conversation, with relaxed shoulders, small natural weight shifts and only her hand gestures. So LEADER is still while she speaks (0.6–2.3 s and 10.7–13.1 s) and dances the rest of the time; HERO is still while she speaks (2.5–9.8 s) and dances before 2.5 s and after 9.8 s. Irregular natural blinking; eyes make tiny tracking movements; the speaker's gaze breaks from the lens briefly, then returns; the listener looks at the speaker. Visible breathing, natural weight and momentum, heads settle after each turn. PINKDRESS and GREYMAN do not dance; they are a couple passing through close behind the group, a small funny moment, about 1–2 m behind LEADER, close enough that their faces and reactions read clearly. 0–3 s: not in frame. About 3 s: they walk into frame from the left, shoulder to shoulder, strolling slowly toward the right, chatting. About 5 s: PINKDRESS laughs and gives GREYMAN's waist a light pat, looking at him. About 6–7.5 s: they keep strolling right, relaxed. About 7.5 s: GREYMAN suddenly notices the camera, a quick surprised look straight at the lens, eyebrows up. About 8 s: he grabs PINKDRESS's hand; both duck their heads and cover their faces with their free hands, like they've been caught, and she giggles behind her hand. About 8–9.5 s: they tiptoe-scurry fast, hunched, out of frame on the right side, sneaking away, still behind LEADER and HERO. After about 9.5 s they are gone and do not come back. They never cover LEADER's or HERO's face. FEATHER dances on her own, slow and flowing: arms floating overhead, long hair swinging, slow swirling half spins that make the feathers fly, swaying like seaweed; later she turns to watch LEADER. SILVERMAN dances loosely the whole time: arms up, hips swaying, strolling with a bounce. Nobody reacts to the bear; the dancers keep dancing as if it is normal. Background people keep moving and drift closer through the take; their attention is on LEADER and HERO, never on the lens (the one exception is GREYMAN's quick glance). They never cover a speaker's face while she talks. When someone speaks, the mouth moves only a little: lips part a few millimetres, relaxed jaw, no wide-open vowels. All real motion, no robotic gestures.
