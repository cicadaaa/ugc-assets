## Prompt
【Generation Goal】 One continuous 14-second handheld 16:9 take, no cuts, that looks filmed by a friend dancing along on a city street: seven friends dancing loosely, two of them talking to the camera. Clean frame: no REC, timestamp, focus brackets, grid, border, subtitles, text.

His look is fixed by @image1: short neatly cropped silver-grey hair close to the head, thin round light-metal glasses, plain black crew-neck tee with no logo or mark, black jeans, black belt, black sneakers with white soles; not the hair, glasses or clothes of @image8. @image9 = LOCATION: the street, buildings, crosswalk and curb only; no people, no furniture. @audio1 = LEADER's voice, saying her two lines: her timbre, accent, pitch, delivery and her pronunciation of "Higgsfield". It belongs to LEADER only. @audio2 = HERO's voice: her timbre, accent, pitch and tone, and in its second half her pronunciation of "Higgsfield" (HIGGS-feeld) and of the prices "nine dollars forty-five" and "one dollar eighty". Do not copy its other words or its timing. It belongs to HERO only.
【Subjects and Relationships】 Exactly seven people, no one else: LEADER, HERO, FEATHER, SLICKED, SILVERMAN, PINKDRESS, GREYMAN. Each outfit belongs only to its owner. HERO stays frame-left of LEADER. The group is tight and close to the camera, like friends hanging out together, not spread across the street: nobody is more than about 3 m from the lens. FEATHER and SLICKED are about 1.5–2 m back, about 1 m apart from each other, just behind LEADER and HERO. PINKDRESS and GREYMAN are a step behind them, about 2.5–3 m back, still clearly visible. SILVERMAN starts at frame-left about 1.5 m back. Everyone drifts a little closer over the take, so the group stays tight around LEADER and HERO.
【Location】 The empty city street of @image9: pale stone cast-iron building fronts with fluted columns and warm-lit shop windows, wet dark asphalt, a white zebra crosswalk, a granite curb. No furniture.
【Event Script】
0.0–0.6 s: LEADER dances right up to the lens, laughing, hips swaying, one arm lifted so her wide sleeve sweeps past the lens, bob swinging; silent.
0.6–2.3 s: LEADER stops dancing, stands still and says "Still paying Higgs-field for this?" to the lens, sly raised eyebrow; the camera steps back to collarbone-up, focus locks on her eyes. HERO soft at frame-left, dancing and watching her.
2.5–4.8 s: the camera pulls back to about 2 m, waist-up, LEADER dancing beside HERO; a quick hand pan to HERO, who stops dancing, stands still and says "We made Higgs-field three x cheaper." toward the lens. LEADER grins at her. Just behind, PINKDRESS and GREYMAN dance facing each other about a metre apart, chatting; FEATHER and SLICKED dance about a metre apart, each in her own style; SILVERMAN dances closer, watching HERO.
5.0–9.8 s: medium-wide at about 1.5 m, HERO stands still, not dancing, and says "I cost nine dollars forty-five on Higgs-field… and now one dollar eighty on treg.", counting on her fingers at chest height; LEADER dances beside her and reacts a beat late with a delighted look.
9.9–10.6 s: SILVERMAN dances across right in front of the lens from left to right, arms loose and swinging, mid-groove, not noticing the camera; his white tee fills and blocks the whole frame for a moment, dark and blurred, autofocus hunts, then he is gone.
10.7–13.1 s: he clears the frame; LEADER leans right into the lens until her face fills the frame, about 0.3 m away, eyes level with the camera, and says "Told you. It's pay per call." with a sly, knowing smirk: a closed-lip smile pulled up on one side, narrowed eyes, one eyebrow slightly raised, chin a little down, like someone who was right all along. No laugh, no open-mouth grin. HERO dancing and laughing soft behind her shoulder.
13.1–13.5 s: still smirking, LEADER reaches toward the camera with an open palm; her hand grows until it fills the whole frame.
13.5–14.0 s: her palm covers the entire lens. The frame is completely black and stays fully black to the last frame: no gaps of light between the fingers, no partial cover, nothing visible.
【Camera and Light】 Fujifilm X100VI 23mm f/2, Eterna film look: low saturation, soft contrast, lifted shadows, fine grain, shallow depth of field. Held in one hand by a friend dancing along: loose bounce on the beat, small tremors, framing that drifts and corrects, a slight horizon tilt he keeps fixing, no gimbal, no smooth gliding; he moves close to whoever speaks and backs off to show the group. Soft overcast late-afternoon daylight around 6500K from a white-grey sky, no direct sun, no hard shadows; shop windows add a small warm 2700K glow in the background only. A light breeze moves hair, sleeves and feathers.
【Performance】 Movement style for everyone, like a group of friends goofing around backstage before a show: loose, carefree, unpolished party dancing, never a routine. Typical moves: hips swaying side to side, shoulders rolling, one or both arms lifted overhead and waving loosely, wrists flicking, a lazy half spin on the spot, walking-while-dancing (strolling a few steps with a bounce, then stopping to groove), playful hand flourishes toward each other, heads tilted back laughing. Moves are big enough to read clearly but relaxed and a little sloppy, each person in their own rhythm, nobody in sync. LEADER and HERO: whenever she is not speaking, each of them dances this way. Whenever she is speaking, she stops dancing and stands still, talking like a normal conversation, with relaxed shoulders, small natural weight shifts and only her hand gestures. So LEADER is still while she speaks (0.6–2.3 s and 10.7–13.1 s) and dances the rest of the time; HERO is still while she speaks (2.5–9.8 s) and dances before 2.5 s and after 9.8 s. Irregular natural blinking; eyes make tiny tracking movements; the speaker's gaze breaks from the lens briefly, then returns; the listener looks at the speaker. Visible breathing, natural weight and momentum, heads settle after each turn. PINKDRESS and GREYMAN stay one step behind FEATHER and SLICKED. 0–5 s: not touching, about a metre apart, facing each other, dancing loosely and chatting, she laughing with a hand raised. 5–8 s: they step closer while dancing. From 8 s: they hug side by side, arms around each other, swaying together and chatting into each other's ears, sometimes a quick look over at LEADER and HERO. They never look into the lens. FEATHER and SLICKED move in two clearly different styles. FEATHER is slow and flowing: arms floating overhead, long hair swinging, slow swirling half spins that make the feathers fly, swaying like seaweed. SLICKED is quick and bouncy: small sharp shoulder pops, little hops on the spot, tight quick steps and finger snaps, elbows close to her body. Their distance changes through the take: 0–4 s they dance about a metre apart, each in her own style, glancing at each other; 4–8 s they drift toward each other while still dancing their own way; 8–10 s they meet, laughing and chatting: FEATHER playfully runs her fingers over SLICKED's slicked-back hair, SLICKED lifts a strand of FEATHER's long black hair and tucks it behind her ear; 10–14 s they spin apart again, still dancing, and both turn to watch LEADER. Gentle and playful, never acrobatic. SILVERMAN dances loosely the whole time: arms up, hips swaying, strolling with a bounce. Background people keep moving and drift closer through the take; their attention is on LEADER and HERO, never on the lens. They never cover a speaker's face while she talks. When someone speaks, the mouth moves only a little: lips part a few millimetres, relaxed jaw, no wide-open vowels. All real motion, no robotic gestures.
