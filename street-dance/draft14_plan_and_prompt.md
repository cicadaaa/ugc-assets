## Prompt

【Generation Goal】 One continuous 14-second handheld 16:9 take, no cuts, that looks filmed by a friend dancing along on a city street: six friends, two of them talking to the camera, and a giant bear walking upright past behind them, twice as big as before, so colossal that only the lower part of its two shaggy legs is ever in frame. Clean frame: no REC, timestamp, focus brackets, grid, border, subtitles, text.
【Reference Asset Roles】 @image1 = the first frame: starting composition, the four people in it, their outfits and the street. PINKDRESS, GREYMAN and the bear are not in it; they enter later. @image2 = LEADER: face, black blunt bob with short fringe, off-white pinstripe yukata with wide charcoal obi only; not its grey background. @image3 = HERO: face, platinum blonde hair, black scoop tank, white pleated trousers, black Mary Janes only; empty hands. @image4 = FEATHER: face, long wavy black hair, lavender feather dress only. @image5 = SILVERMAN: face, shoulder-length silver hair, white crew tee, beige chinos, black belt only. In each cast image use only the face on the left; the right panels show clothes only. @image6 = PINKDRESS: face, long wavy chestnut-brown hair, pink sleeveless V-neck fit-and-flare mini dress, pearl drop earrings, nude pumps only. @image7 = GREYMAN: face only. He is a short man, about 165 cm, his head about level with PINKDRESS's eyes. He is not in the first frame, so his look comes from this text: short neatly cropped silver-grey hair close to the head, thin round light-metal glasses, plain black crew-neck tee with no logo or mark, black jeans, black belt, black sneakers with white soles; not the hair, glasses or clothes of @image7. @image8 = LOCATION: the street, buildings, crosswalk and curb only; no people, no furniture. @audio1 = LEADER's voice: her timbre, accent, pitch and delivery, and at its end her pronunciation of "Higgsfield" (HIGGS-feeld). It belongs to LEADER only. @audio2 = HERO's voice: her timbre, accent, pitch and tone, and in its second half her pronunciation of "Higgsfield" (HIGGS-feeld) and of the prices "nine dollars forty-five" and "one dollar eighty". Do not copy its other words or its timing. It belongs to HERO only. @audio3 = pronunciation guide only, in HERO's voice: exactly how to say "Higgsfield" (HIGGS-feeld) and "treg" (TREG, rhymes with egg and Greg). Copy only how these two words sound; do not copy any other words, timing or delivery from them.
【Subjects and Relationships】 Six people, no one else: LEADER, HERO, FEATHER, SILVERMAN, PINKDRESS, GREYMAN, plus one giant bear. Each outfit belongs only to its owner. HERO stays frame-left of LEADER. The group is tight and close to the camera, like friends hanging out together, not spread across the street: nobody is more than about 3 m from the lens. FEATHER is about 1.5–2 m back, just behind LEADER and HERO. PINKDRESS and GREYMAN are not in frame at the start; they walk in from the left at about 3 s, close behind the group, about 1–2 m behind LEADER, and walk across to the right. The bear walks the same path about 5 m further back than the couple, behind everyone. SILVERMAN starts at frame-left about 1.5 m back. The dancers drift a little closer over the take, so the group stays tight around LEADER and HERO.
【The Bear】 A colossal brown bear, about twice as big as a giant bear already taller than the buildings: it towers far above the rooftops, walking upright on its two hind legs, like a person. Each leg alone is as thick as a building column cluster and wider than a car, each foot as long as a car. It is so enormous that only the lower part of its two legs, from the shin down, fits in the frame; even the knees are above the top edge of the frame. Its head, body, belly and front paws are never seen. Its fur is extremely long and shaggy, about twice as long as a normal bear's, hanging down in thick dark-brown locks that sway, bounce and shake heavily with every step. No claws are visible: the long fur covers the feet, and only the soft dark sole of a foot shows for a moment when it lifts. It strides from left to right, the two legs crossing one in front of the other like someone walking past, each foot landing flat with its full weight on the wet asphalt; puddles ripple and splash a little. It ignores everyone and keeps walking.
【Location】 The empty city street of @image8: pale stone cast-iron building fronts with fluted columns and warm-lit shop windows, wet dark asphalt, a white zebra crosswalk, a granite curb. No furniture.
【Event Script】
0.0–0.6 s: LEADER dances right up to the lens, laughing, hips swaying, bob swinging, and swings one arm in a quick wave right in front of the camera so her wide pinstripe sleeve sweeps across the lens and actually brushes against it: for a split second the soft out-of-focus striped fabric fills the frame and the camera gives a small bump, then the sleeve slides off and her face is back, still laughing from the dance; silent.
0.6–2.3 s: LEADER slows to a stop, her laugh settling into a teasing half-smile, and says "Still paying Higgs-field for this?" to the lens, one eyebrow lifting on "this"; the camera steps back to collarbone-up, focus locks on her eyes. HERO soft at frame-left, dancing and watching her.
2.5–4.8 s: the camera pulls back to about 2 m, waist-up, LEADER dancing beside HERO; a quick hand pan to HERO, who has been laughing at LEADER; she lets her last dance step finish, turns to the lens with a proud little grin and says "We made Higgs-field three x cheaper.", a small shrug on "cheaper". LEADER glances at her and grins, still dancing. At about 3 s PINKDRESS and GREYMAN walk into frame from the left, about 1–2 m behind LEADER, shoulder to shoulder, strolling slowly toward the right; FEATHER dances on her own; SILVERMAN dances closer, watching HERO.
5.0–9.8 s: medium-wide at about 1.5 m, HERO stands still, not dancing, and says "I cost nine dollars forty-five on Higgs-field… and now one dollar eighty on treg.", counting on her fingers at chest height: mock-serious and a little pained on "nine dollars forty-five", then her eyebrows go up and she breaks into a pleased smile on "one dollar eighty". LEADER dances beside her, looks at her on the second price and nods with a delighted look, a beat late. At about 7.5 s GREYMAN notices the camera; he and PINKDRESS shield their faces a little with a hand, turn away and walk off quickly out of frame-right by about 9.5 s, in one natural, continuous motion. Only these two react to the camera; SILVERMAN keeps dancing and grinning. From about 5.5 s the bear's two giant long-haired legs stride into frame from the left, upright, behind the couple, crossing one in front of the other, the long fur shaking with each step; each step lands with a deep thud and the camera gives a tiny jolt. The bear keeps striding right.
9.9–10.6 s: SILVERMAN dances across right in front of the lens from left to right, arms loose and swinging, mid-groove, not noticing the camera; his white tee fills and blocks the whole frame for a moment, dark and blurred, autofocus hunts, then he is gone.
10.7–13.1 s: he clears the frame; LEADER, who was already stepping toward the camera behind him, leans right into the lens until her face fills the frame, about 0.3 m away, eyes level with the camera, and says "Told you. It's pay per call." with a sly, knowing smirk: a closed-lip smile pulled up on one side, narrowed eyes, one eyebrow slightly raised, chin a little down, like someone who was right all along. No laugh, no open-mouth grin. HERO, done talking, is back to dancing, relaxed and smiling, soft behind her shoulder. Behind them, soft and out of focus, the bear's last shaggy leg lifts and strides out of frame-right by about 12 s.
13.1–13.5 s: still smirking, LEADER reaches toward the camera with an open palm; her hand grows until it fills the whole frame.
13.5–14.0 s: her palm covers the entire lens. The frame is completely black and stays fully black to the last frame: no gaps of light between the fingers, no partial cover, nothing visible.
【Camera and Light】 Fujifilm X100VI 23mm f/2, Eterna film look: low saturation, soft contrast, lifted shadows, fine grain, shallow depth of field. Held in one hand by a friend dancing along: loose bounce on the beat, small tremors, framing that drifts and corrects, a slight horizon tilt he keeps fixing, no gimbal, no smooth gliding; he moves close to whoever speaks and backs off to show the group. While the bear walks, each of its steps gives the camera a tiny jolt. Soft overcast late-afternoon daylight around 6500K from a white-grey sky, no direct sun, no hard shadows; shop windows add a small warm 2700K glow in the background only. A light breeze moves hair, sleeves and feathers.

Whenever she is speaking, she stops dancing and stands still, talking like a normal conversation, with relaxed shoulders, small natural weight shifts and only her hand gestures. Visible breathing, natural weight and momentum, heads settle after each turn.
